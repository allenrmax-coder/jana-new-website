Accessibility Statement

Last updated: May 27, 2026

Mara Hair and Mode Studio is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards to this website and to the booking process.

Conformance status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) defines requirements to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities. This site aims to conform with WCAG 2.1 level AA. Some areas of the site partially conform — we are actively working to bring everything into full conformance.

Measures we take

  • Use semantic HTML and clear page structure so assistive technologies can navigate the site.
  • Provide meaningful alternative text for images that convey information.
  • Maintain color-contrast ratios that meet WCAG AA requirements for body text.
  • Support full keyboard navigation for interactive elements (links, buttons, the booking modal, and forms).
  • Honor user motion preferences — animations are reduced when the operating system requests prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Avoid relying on color alone to convey meaning.
  • Review and test new pages with screen readers and keyboard navigation before publishing.

Known limitations

Some content on this site may not yet be fully accessible. We are aware of the following limitations:

  • Our booking system is operated by a third-party provider (Meevo). We are working with them to confirm their compliance status.
  • Some embedded social media content may not meet the same accessibility standards as our own content.
  • Older photography may not have detailed alt text.

Accessibility at the studio

Our physical studio at 4011 Chain Bridge Rd, Fairfax, VA, is committed to welcoming all guests. If you have specific accessibility needs for an in-person visit — including mobility, sensory, or chemical-sensitivity accommodations — please contact us in advance and we will do our best to make arrangements.

Feedback & contact

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of this site and our studio. If you encounter an accessibility barrier, or you need information in an alternative format, please let us know — we aim to respond within 5 business days.
