Our Story

A Studio Built on
Artistry and Soul

Founded in 2015 by Jana Klavina, Mara Hair and Mode Studio was one of the first lifestyle boutiques in the greater DC area. What began as a singular vision for a different kind of salon experience has grown into an award-winning studio home to 15 artists who share one belief:

"Great hair isn't just beauty — it's the boldest form of self-expression."

Trained at Bumble and Bumble in NYC, Jana built this studio on the values of minimal, intentional change that creates profound confidence. Five-time recipient of Best Salon — Northern Virginia Magazine (2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2025).

House Rules

Studio Policies

  • A valid credit card is required at the time of booking.
  • Book online or by phone at 703.591.9099.
  • All appointments must be confirmed 48 hours in advance — unconfirmed appointments may be cancelled.
  • Incorrect service bookings result in a 50% cancellation fee.
  • Consultations are available for $15, which is applied toward your service.
  • 48 hours' notice is required for cancellations or rescheduling.
  • Late cancellations or no-shows incur a 50% charge of the total services booked.
  • Monday appointments must be cancelled by Saturday.
  • If running late, contact the studio at 571.992.0407.
  • Your stylist may need to adjust or reschedule services depending on availability.
  • Cancellations due to lateness are subject to a 50% charge.
  • Face masks are optional; available upon request.
  • No outside food in the studio; beverages are welcome.
  • Please arrive alone unless you are an adult accompanying a minor.
  • Cash is preferred for gratuity; Venmo is also accepted.
  • Gratuity cannot be added to credit card payments.
  • If you're unsatisfied, you have two weeks to request an adjustment with your original stylist.
  • After two weeks or with a different stylist, the service is considered new.
  • Bumble & Bumble products may be exchanged within 60 days if unopened or lightly used.
  • Returns are not currently offered.
