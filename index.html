Our Story

A Studio Built on
Artistry and Soul

Founded in 2015 by Jana Klavina, Mara Hair and Mode Studio was one of the first lifestyle boutiques in the greater DC area. What began as a singular vision for a different kind of salon experience has grown into an award-winning studio of 15 artists who share one belief:

"Great hair isn't just beauty — it's the boldest form of self-expression."

Trained at Bumble and Bumble in NYC, Jana built this studio on the values of minimal, intentional change that creates profound confidence. Five-time recipient of Best Salon — Northern Virginia Magazine (2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2025).

Mara Hair Studio

house rules

Our policies aim to provide a seamless and consistent experience for our valued clients while also acknowledging the efforts and dedication of our employees. We sincerely appreciate your understanding!

  • A valid credit card is required at the time of booking an appointment.
  • You can book appointments by calling us or booking online via our website.
  • If you don't see your stylist or the services you're looking for online, please call us. Please do not book for a different service than what you'd like to get done.
  • If you're unsure of what services you'd like, please call us directly. Booking the incorrect service will result in a 50% cancellation fee if your stylist is unable to perform the service you need for your desired look.
  • All appointments must be confirmed at least 48 hours in advance through our automated text or email system.
  • If your appointment isn't confirmed, it will be subject to cancellation to allow other clients the opportunity to book in.
  • Didn't get a confirmation message? Please contact us directly to fix any issues on our end.
  • Please notify us 48 hours prior to your appointment if you need to cancel or reschedule.
  • Late cancellations or missed appointments will incur a 50% charge of the total service(s) to the credit card on file.
  • If your appointment falls on a Monday, kindly let us know by Saturday, as we're closed on Sundays.
  • If you happen to be running late, please give us a quick call or send a text at 571-992-0407. We understand that life happens!
  • Your stylist may need to adjust or reschedule your appointment accordingly to respect the rest of their clients' time for the day.
  • Unfortunately, if your appointment needs to be canceled due to lateness, a 50% charge of the total service(s) will be applied to the card on file.
  • Face masks are optional, but if you prefer your stylist to wear one during your appointment, just let us know.
  • To maintain a clean and pleasant environment, we kindly ask that you refrain from bringing outside food. However, drinks are absolutely fine!
  • When arriving for your appointment, we request that you come alone, except when an adult needs to accompany a minor who cannot be left unattended.
  • Should you feel inspired to leave gratuity, cash is preferred. We also accept Venmo — stylists' Venmos are listed on their profiles.
  • Please note that gratuity cannot be left via credit cards.
  • If for any reason you're not fully satisfied with your new hairstyle, please reach out to us within two weeks.
  • The stylist who performed the original service will gladly make adjustments to ensure your desired look.
  • After two weeks, or if you choose to see a different stylist, it is considered a new service.
  • If you're not thrilled with a Bumble and Bumble product, we offer exchanges within 60 days of purchase for unopened or lightly used items.
  • Currently, we do not offer returns.
  • The closest free public lot is behind Truist Bank on Sager Avenue.
  • We have limited designated parking at the back of the building — access it through the alleyway between Mara Hair and Mode Studio and the Old Town Fairfax Building.
  • These spots are shared with the neighboring restaurant and are in high demand, so we recommend planning ahead.
  • Please stay in touch! Don't hesitate to reach out via text to our texting line at 571-992-0407.
  • Our front desk staff is here to answer any questions you might have.
