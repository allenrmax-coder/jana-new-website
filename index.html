Our Story

A Studio Built on
Artistry and Soul

Founded in 2015 by Jana Klavina, Mara Hair and Mode Studio was one of the first lifestyle boutiques in the greater DC area. What began as a singular vision for a different kind of salon experience has grown into an award-winning studio of 15 artists who share one belief:

"Great hair isn't just beauty — it's the boldest form of self-expression."

Trained at Bumble and Bumble in NYC, Jana built this studio on the values of minimal, intentional change that creates profound confidence. Five-time recipient of Best Salon — Northern Virginia Magazine (2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2025).

House Rules

Studio Policies

  • A valid credit card is required at the time of booking.
  • Book online or by phone at 703.591.9099.
  • All appointments must be confirmed 48 hours in advance — unconfirmed appointments may be cancelled.
  • Incorrect service bookings result in a 50% cancellation fee.
  • Consultations available for $15 — applied toward your service.
  • 48 hours' notice required for cancellations or rescheduling.
  • Late cancellations or no-shows incur a 50% charge of the total services booked.
  • Monday appointments must be cancelled by Saturday.
  • If running late, contact the studio at 571.992.0407.
  • Your stylist may need to adjust or reschedule based on availability.
  • Cancellations due to lateness are subject to a 50% charge.
  • Face masks are optional; available upon request.
  • No outside food; beverages are welcome.
  • Please arrive alone unless accompanying a minor.
  • Cash is preferred; Venmo also accepted.
  • Gratuity cannot be added to credit card payments.
  • Two weeks to request an adjustment with your original stylist if unsatisfied.
  • After two weeks or with a different stylist — considered a new service.
  • Bumble & Bumble products: exchanges within 60 days if unopened or lightly used.
  • Returns not currently offered.
