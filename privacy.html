Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

Mara Hair and Mode Studio ("Mara," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or book an appointment with us.

Information we collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact information you provide to us — your name, phone number, email address, and any message content — when you call, text, email, or fill out a form on our site.
  • Appointment information — service requested, preferred stylist, preferred date/time, and notes you choose to share — which is processed by our third-party booking provider (Meevo).
  • Automatically collected information — basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and approximate location, gathered by standard web analytics tools.

How we use your information

  • To schedule, confirm, and follow up on appointments.
  • To respond to inquiries about our services.
  • To send appointment reminders and, where you have opted in, occasional promotional updates.
  • To improve our website, services, and customer experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our legal rights.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate the business, including:

  • Meevo — our booking and salon management platform.
  • Hosting and analytics providers (such as our website host, Google Fonts, and analytics tools) for basic site functionality and usage measurement.
  • Payment processors for any transactions made on our premises.
  • Legal and regulatory bodies when required by law.

Cookies and tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and measure traffic. You can control cookies in your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the site.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Virginia residents and California residents have specific rights under the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) respectively, including the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by law. Appointment records may be retained for tax, accounting, and service-history purposes.

Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of the page reflects when changes were made. Material changes will be communicated through our site.
